What is the WBO Tech Suite?

Working by Outcomes (WBO) is a cornerstone element of our operating model, essential for realizing the One Pharma Strategy (OPS). By enabling greater focus and collaboration, WBO aims to minimize "energetic cost" while maximizing positive patient, societal, and business impact.

The WBO Tech Suite is the technology platform that makes this framework live and actionable. It replaces a fragmented ecosystem of Google Slides, Sheets, Trello, and Tableau with a single "one source of truth" on monday.com, with Airtable used by affiliates in the Middle East and North Africa.

💡 The Tech Suite is not just another tool — it's your engine for growth and global collaboration.

📋 Key Strategic Documents

Access the foundational strategy documents for your Affiliate

DACS

Disease Area Commercialisation Strategy

  • Defines the commercial strategy at the portfolio / disease area level across the affiliate
  • Sets the strategic direction for multiple products within a therapeutic area
  • Typically covers a 3-year strategic horizon

CORP

Commercialisation Outcomes Realisation Plan

  • Translates the DACS into an executable operational plan at the product level
  • Focuses on a specific product with concrete outcomes and work packages
  • Typically operates on a 1-year cycle aligned with planning rhythms
